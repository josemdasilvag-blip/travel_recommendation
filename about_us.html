About us

A small studio that plans trips like a friend would

Wanderlynx Travel was founded in Barcelona in 2011 by three people who were tired of itineraries written by algorithms that had never left the office. We are a team of eleven travel designers, and every destination we recommend has been visited by at least one of us. We do not sell packages: we sell time well spent.

Guidance for team members

Every Wanderlynx travel designer follows the same five working rules. New team members are expected to read them during their first week and to apply them to every recommendation they publish.

  1. Visit before you recommend. A destination only enters the catalogue after a team member has been there in the last three years.
  2. Write the drawback. Every description must name one honest downside — crowds, price, a long transfer. If there is none, look harder.
  3. Answer within one working day. Traveller messages that arrive through the Contact Us form are assigned the same morning and answered within twenty-four hours.
  4. Respect the destination. Recommend local operators, mention dress codes and opening hours for religious sites, never send groups larger than eight to fragile places.
  5. Keep the data clean. Photos, descriptions and time zones live in travel_recommendation_api.json. Update that file, open a pull request, and never edit the live site by hand.
